Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to some of the questions we receive most often.

Can I schedule a campus tour?

Yes. Simply contact our admissions office or complete the contact form above to arrange a convenient date and time.

How long does it take to receive a response?

We typically respond to all enquiries within one business day.

Can I visit the school without an appointment?

Walk-in visits are welcome during office hours, although booking ahead ensures a member of our admissions team is available to assist you.

Where can I download the school prospectus?

You can download our prospectus from the Admissions page or request a printed copy during your visit.

Welcome to Greenfield Admissions

Choosing the right school is one of the most important decisions a family can make. At Greenfield International Academy, we are committed to making the admission process welcoming, transparent, and straightforward for every parent and student.

Our admissions team is here to guide you through every step, answer your questions, and help you discover how Greenfield can provide your child with an environment where they can learn, grow, and thrive.

Frequently Asked Questions

What age can my child begin school?

Admission begins from our Early Years programme based on the school's admission policy.

Do you provide school transportation?

Yes. Safe and reliable transportation is available for selected routes.

Are scholarships available?

Yes. Merit-based scholarships are available for qualified students.

Can students transfer during the school year?

Yes, subject to available spaces and successful completion of the admission process.

Which curriculum does Greenfield follow?

We offer a well-balanced curriculum designed to prepare students for academic excellence and global opportunities.
